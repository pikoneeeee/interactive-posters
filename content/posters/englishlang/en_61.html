🗣️ Useful Phrases

Customer Waiter
I’d like to order, please. Sure. What would you like?
Can I have the menu? Of course. Here you are.
Can I have some water? Yes, certainly.
Can I have the bill, please? Yes. I’ll bring it right away.

💬 Mini Dialogue

Waiter: Hello! Can I help you?
Customer: Yes, can I see the menu, please?
Waiter: Here you are. What would you like to order?
Customer: I’d like pasta and orange juice.
Waiter: Great choice! Anything else?
Customer: No, thank you.
