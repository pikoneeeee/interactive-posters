SHOPPING & STORES

Buying things and talking in shops

🏬 Types of Stores

Supermarket
Big food store
I buy food at the supermarket.
Clothes shop
Sells clothes
She works in a clothes shop.
Bookshop
Sells books
I like this bookshop.
Market
Open-air shop
We buy fruit at the market.

🛍️ Shopping Words

Price
Cost of something
What is the price?
Cash / Card
Ways to pay
I pay by card.
Receipt
Paper after paying
Here is your receipt.
Discount
Lower price
There is a discount.

🗣️ Useful Phrases

Customer Shop assistant
How much is this? It’s ten dollars.
Can I try it on? Yes, of course.
Do you have another size? Yes, here you are.
I’ll take it. Cash or card?

💬 Mini Dialogue

Customer: Excuse me, how much is this T-shirt?
Assistant: It’s $15.
Customer: Can I try it on?
Assistant: Yes, the fitting room is there.
Customer: Great, I’ll take it.
Assistant: Cash or card?
